Immich GeoTagger adds location information to photos from cameras without GPS.

Start tracking before taking photos, keep your phone with you, and import the photos into Immich as usual. GeoTagger compares each photo's capture time with your recorded route, calculates a matching location, and shows you a preview before anything is changed.

You can review thumbnails, deselect individual photos, and only apply the locations you approve. Photos that already contain GPS information are left untouched.

Important: your camera and phone must use the same time and time zone so capture times can be matched correctly.

Location tracking is performed locally on your device. The Android tracking stack uses the standard AOSP LocationManager and does not depend on Google Play Services.
